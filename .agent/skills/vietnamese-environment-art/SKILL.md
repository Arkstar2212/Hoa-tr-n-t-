# Vietnamese Historical & Cultural Environment Art (1950s Nam Bộ)

This skill provides cultural, historical, and geographical guidelines for world-building, prop placement, and level design in **Hoa trên đất đỏ**.

## 1. Setting Context: Cà Mau & Nam Bộ (circa 1954)

- **Geography**: Mekong Delta / U Minh / Cà Mau peninsula — a maze of rivers, canals, brackish mangroves, and vast alluvial plains.
- **Atmosphere**: The quiet, humid breath of the Southern wetlands; sounds of wind in the nipa palms, distant boat oars, cicadas, and rustling reeds.
- **Soil & Earth**:
  - Alluvial red-brown silt (đất phù sa màu mỡ) along riverbanks.
  - Basalt red dirt roads (đường đất đỏ Ba-zan) leading inland.
  - Muddy tidal shores exposed at low tide with crab holes and mangrove breathing roots.

---

## 2. Flora, Architecture & Prop Reference

### Authentic Flora (Thực vật bản địa)
- **Cây dừa nước (Nipa palm)**: Lining every canal and waterway. Thick clusters of arching fronds rising directly from mud.
- **Rừng tràm (Melaleuca woods)**: Slender grey-white bark trees rising out of black peat water.
- **Cây bần, cây đước (Mangrove trees)**: Arching aerial stilt roots along tidal estuaries.
- **Ruộng lúa (Rice fields)**: Lush green when young, shimmering golden yellow when ripe, partitioned by low earthen dykes (bờ ruộng).
- **Tre gai, chuối nước, bèo tây (Water hyacinths)**: Floating rafts of purple-flowered hyacinths drifting along river currents.

### Authentic Architecture & Structures (Kiến trúc & Đời sống)
- **Nhà mái lá dừa nước**: Thatched roofs woven from dried nipa palm leaves; walls made of earthen wattle or unpainted weathered timber planks.
- **Bến đò & Cầu khỉ**: Simple wooden river docks, bamboo monkey bridges spanning small canals.
- **Ghe & Xuồng ba lá**: Traditional Southern three-plank wooden canoes propelled with long single oars or steering paddles.
- **Everyday Props**: Chum vại sành sứt mẻ chứa nước mưa, ngọn đèn dầu hỏa hoa kỳ hun khói, nón lá ngả màu rơm, võng gai mắc hiên nhà, củi đước bó gọn.

## 3. Critical Anachronisms & Tropes to Avoid

| Prohibited Trope | Why It Is Wrong | Correct Alternative |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Japanese/East Asian Tropes** | Torii gates, cherry blossoms, tatami mats are culturally alien. | Bamboo groves, hoa sen (lotus), hoa súng, wooden benches. |
| **European / Alpine Pines** | European conifers do not exist in the tropical Southern delta. | Tràm (Melaleuca), dừa nước (Nipa palm), cây bần. |
| **High Fantasy Ruins** | Stone castles, glowing magical obelisks destroy the historical poignancy. | Abandoned wooden outposts, overgrown earthen dykes, weathered shrines. |
| **Modern Concrete & Plastic** | Concrete sea walls, corrugated metal sheets, plastic containers did not exist in 1950s rural Nam Bộ. | Wood pylons, dried thatch, ceramic water urns, woven bamboo baskets. |

---

## 4. Scene Composition Checklist for Level Design

1. Does the waterways look natural with irregular earthen banks and mangrove roots rather than straight modern canal walls?
2. Are wooden river docks weathered with water stains and moss?
3. Are props (xuồng, nón lá, đèn dầu, chum nước) positioned realistically to convey human life and habitation?
4. Does the background silhouetting suggest sprawling flat delta horizons rather than alpine jagged cliffs?
